# Sendora name and logo

The code of Sendora is free software under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)
and [LICENSE-ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md](LICENSE-ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md)). The software license
covers the code; it does not cover the name "Sendora" or the Sendora logo. This short
policy says how you may use them. The aim is simple: people should be able to tell
the official project apart from other versions, and nobody should be misled.

## You may, without asking

- **Run and redistribute unmodified builds** of Sendora under its name, including
  the official container images, with the name and logo as shipped.
- **Refer to the project** by name, for example "runs on Sendora", "a fork of
  Sendora", "compatible with Sendora", or in articles, reviews, tutorials and talks.
- **Keep the "Powered by Sendora" attribution** in the interface, as required by
  the additional terms, including in modified versions.
- Make small changes for your own deployment (configuration, translations, your own
  logo and application name through the branding settings) and keep calling the
  software Sendora when you describe what you run.

## Forks and modified versions

If you distribute a modified version, or offer it to others as a service, give it
**a different name and logo**: do not use "Sendora" as, or as part of, the name of
your product, and do not use the Sendora logo as its logo.

At the same time a fork **must keep** the "Powered by Sendora" attribution with its
link to the project, as required by term 1 of
[LICENSE-ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md](LICENSE-ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md). Renaming and keeping
the attribution go together: your product carries your name, and "Powered by
Sendora" says where it comes from. You may also say in words that it is based on
Sendora.

## Not allowed

- Suggesting that the Sendora project or its maintainers endorse, sponsor or support
  your product, service or company when they do not.
- Using the name or logo in a way that is confusing, misleading or disparaging, or
  as part of your own trademark, company name or domain name.

The required "Powered by Sendora" attribution (and accurate statements such as
"based on Sendora") is always allowed and never counts as a breach of the points
above: it is not a product name, not an implied endorsement and not a use of the
name as your own mark. Nothing in this policy requires or allows removing it.

## Questions

Unsure whether your use is fine? Open an issue at
<https://github.com/sendora-app/sendora/issues> and ask. This policy may be updated;
it does not limit any right you have under the software license or the law (for
example, fair or nominative use).
